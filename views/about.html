MY STORY

Hi, I'm Hyatt

Photography has been my lifelong passion. What started as a curious kid with a disposable camera has grown into a career dedicated to capturing the world's most beautiful moments.

Based in Australia, I've had the privilege of photographing weddings across the country's most stunning venues, capturing portraits that reveal the soul, and documenting landscapes that take your breath away.

My approach is simple: I believe in authentic moments over posed perfection. I seek the genuine emotion, the unscripted laughter, and the quiet intimacy that makes each story unique.
